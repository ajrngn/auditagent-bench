Reading Task C. The false-positive rate is the share of genuinely clean Item 9A disclosures a model incorrectly flags as deficient. Lower is better, and it is the metric that determines whether these models are usable for real control review. Paraphrased Δ is the score change on the paraphrased subset versus verbatim filing text — a large gap indicates memorization rather than reasoning.
